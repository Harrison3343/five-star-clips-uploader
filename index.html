Five Star Clips Uploader

A personal tool that uploads my own short videos to my own channels.

What it does

Five Star Clips Uploader runs on my own computer. It takes short vertical videos I have made and uploads them to the Five Star Clips YouTube channel and my TikTok account on a schedule I set.

  • YouTube: uploads videos through the official YouTube Data API.
  • TikTok: sends videos to my TikTok drafts through the official Content Posting API. I finish and post each one myself in the TikTok app.

Who can use it

Nobody else. It is not a public service and there is no sign-up. It only ever signs in to accounts I own. It does not access, collect or post on behalf of anyone else.

Your data

Login tokens are stored only on my own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than YouTube and TikTok, and no data is shared with anyone. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
