Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Five Star Clips Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool. It runs only on its owner's own computer and uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Information the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): permission to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read basic channel details (youtube.readonly). The read permission is only used to confirm which channel is signed in.
  • TikTok: basic profile information (user.info.basic) and permission to send videos to the account's drafts (video.upload).
  • The video files and captions the owner has created on their own computer.

How it is used

This information is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's accounts. The app does not read, collect or analyse anyone else's data. It does not use data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

Login tokens (OAuth credentials) are stored only on the owner's computer, in the app's local folder. They are never uploaded anywhere. There is no server, database, analytics or tracking, and the app sets no cookies.

Sharing

The app shares no data with anyone. Data is only sent to Google/YouTube and TikTok, and only to perform the uploads the owner requests.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until they are deleted from the app's folder or access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Google/YouTube: Google Account third-party access settings
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions, in the TikTok app

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok

TikTok's handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from them.
